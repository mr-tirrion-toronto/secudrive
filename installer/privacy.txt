SECUDRIVE PRIVACY NOTICE

Short version: SecuDrive is a local security tool.  It does not sell your
data and it does not send your scanned files anywhere.

WHAT WE COLLECT
- Locally: scan findings, quarantine records and an audit log are stored in a
  local database (secudrive.db) and encrypted backups under your user profile.
  Nothing from this database leaves your computer unless you choose to use a
  signature feed of your own.
- Online (optional): when you press "Update Signatures" and an online security
  feed is configured, the Software downloads malware signature hashes from
  that feed.  It does not upload your files or scan results.
- Diagnostics: a local debug log (secudrive_debug.log) records GUI actions so
  support issues can be diagnosed.  It stays on your machine.

THIRD PARTIES
- The Software has no built-in telemetry or advertising.  If you configure a
  third-party signature feed, that feed's own privacy policy applies to your
  connection to it.

YOUR CHOICES
- Automatic signature updates and scans can be disabled in the Dashboard.
- Quarantined items can be restored or permanently deleted from the
  Quarantine manager.
- Uninstalling the Software (via the uninstaller) removes its data directory
  unless you keep your encrypted backups deliberately.
